What happens when you delete your account?

We take your privacy seriously. When you submit a deletion request, we will permanently erase the following within 30 days:

  • Your account (email, name, profile picture)
  • All notes, todos, habits, focus sessions, and expenses
  • Your AI chat history and message content
  • Any files or images you attached in-app
  • Session tokens and cached data on our servers
⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered.

What we may keep (anonymized / legal)

  • Anonymized crash logs and usage aggregates (no personal identifiers)
  • Records required by law (up to 6 months) — e.g. payment receipts if any
  • Backups on encrypted archival storage (auto-purged within 60 days)

Alternative: In-App Deletion

You can also delete your data directly from the DailyHub AI app:

  1. Open the DailyHub AI app on your phone
  2. Go to Profile tab (bottom right)
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Sign Out
  4. Then email us at support@shivaminnovation.dev with subject "Delete My Account"

Guest users (who never signed in with Google) — your data lives only on your device. Simply uninstall the app or clear app data from Android Settings to delete everything.
